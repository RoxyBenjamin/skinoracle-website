Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

SkinOracle is a skincare companion that puts you first, and that starts with your information. This policy covers the SkinOracle iPhone app, your account on this website (skinoracle.ai/app), the waitlist and the beta application. It’s written in plain English on purpose. If anything is unclear, write to hello@skinoracle.ai.

The short version

  • Your face photos stay on your iPhone unless you choose to send one. SkinOracle looks at them on your phone with its own rules and never sends them to any AI. If you send a photo to your Friends, it is locked on your iPhone first, so only the friends you picked can open it. We can’t.
  • Your health details stay yours. On the iPhone they stay on the phone (apart from the skin type and concerns you let Ask SkinOracle™ use, below), unless you choose to mirror your iPhone to your computer: then they’re locked on your iPhone first and copied to your own health vault. On this website they go into the same health vault, locked in your browser before anything is sent. Either way, only you can unlock it, not even SkinOracle.
  • We never sell your personal information. Not your name, email, phone, birthday, photos or health details. Ever.
  • We learn about products, never about you. How products are used is recorded without your name or account (more below).
  • You can delete your account in the app, any time: on the iPhone, You › SkinOracle account › Delete my account; on this website, Privacy and your data › Delete my account.

On your iPhone

  • Face photos and your Photo Diary are kept on your iPhone. SkinOracle looks at them on the phone with its own rules to track your skin, and never sends them to any AI. A photo leaves your iPhone only if you choose to send it to your Friends (below) or save a copy somewhere yourself.
  • Friends photo sharing: if you send a photo (of you or a product) to friends you invited, your iPhone shrinks it, strips hidden details like its location, and locks it, together with any caption or details you choose to add (like the date, place, weather or a note), before it’s uploaded. Only the friends you picked can unlock it: we store only the locked copy and can’t see it. Shared photos are deleted after 30 days. Your friends see the display name you choose for Friends and the groups you’re in.
  • Apple Health: if you allow it, SkinOracle reads your sleep, cycle and water from Apple Health on your iPhone to shape your routine, and never writes anything to it. It never goes to SkinOracle’s AI engine or into product records. It stays on your iPhone, and it’s copied to your own health vault only if you choose to mirror your iPhone to your computer (next item): locked on your iPhone first, so only you can unlock it, not even SkinOracle. Your choice, and you can switch it off anytime.
  • Health vault on the iPhone (off unless you turn it on): it comes to the iPhone app in a later update. If you then choose to mirror your iPhone to your computer, your health details (like your life stage, medicines, cycle, check-ins, patterns and trips, and the sleep, cycle and water SkinOracle reads from Apple Health if you turned Health on) are locked on your iPhone first and copied to your health vault, so they show on this website too. We store only the locked copy and can’t read it. The notes you write in a check-in on your iPhone stay on your iPhone. You can switch it off anytime.
  • Camera: used to take your face photos (kept on the phone unless you send one to your Friends) and to photograph a product. For a product, only a cut-out of the product and its label is used, never a person or the background.
  • Product photos: to read a label you scan, the cut-out product photo may be sent to SkinOracle’s AI engine. It never shows a person.
  • Location: if you allow it, your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) is sent to our server to get your local weather from Apple’s weather service. We don’t keep your location; product records keep only your state or region and country.
  • Your shelf, routines and check-ins are kept on your iPhone, and your check-ins and routine log are copied into your health vault only if you choose to mirror (above). What leaves the phone about your products is described under “Products, not people” below.

On this website (your account at skinoracle.ai/app)

  • Health vault: health details you add here (like your skin type and concerns, life stage, cycle, medicines, life events and check-ins), and anything your iPhone mirrors into it if you chose that, are locked with a key only your vault password, recovery code or passkey can open. On this website they’re locked in your browser before anything is sent. We store only the locked copy and can’t read it, or reset your password for you.
  • Photo Diary and the Ask SkinOracle™ chat are kept only in this browser (the chat for 30 days). Clearing your browser’s site data removes them.
  • “Sync my shelf” (off unless you turn it on) keeps your shelf and a few preferences on our server so they match across your devices.
  • Gift codes you claim are recorded so your friend gets their free days.

Friends chat and Circles (on the iPhone and this website)

  • Friends group chat: messages you send are kept for 30 days so your group can read them. Chat messages aren’t locked like shared photos: our safety team can read a chat after it is reported.
  • Circles: posts and replies you write are shown to other members under the Circles name and colour you choose, not your real name or email. We keep them, with your account, so they can be shown, and so our moderators can check posts (your first posts and anything reported may be held for a review) and keep Circles safe. Reports you make and what moderators do are kept too. When you delete your account, your posts become “Deleted member” with their words removed.

Ask SkinOracle™ (the AI chat)

When you ask a question, we send your question, the recent chat, the notes SkinOracle remembers and anonymous facts like your skin type, your shelf, your local weather and the time to SkinOracle’s AI engine to write the answer. Your name and email are never sent, and the request isn’t tied to your account. Photos are never sent, and nothing is added from your health vault or Apple Health. What you type yourself is sent as you wrote it, so leave out anything you’d rather not share. You decide whether to use it, and you can switch it off any time in Privacy. Warning signs always get a fixed “please see someone” answer from SkinOracle’s own safety rules, not from AI.

Products, not people

SkinOracle learns which skincare products actually work for people. To do that, the app and this website record information about products, sent without your name, email, phone, account or any device serial number:

  • Product details: barcode, name, brand, type, size, ingredients, the text on the label, and prices and stores we find.
  • How products are used: when a product is added, opened, used, paused, finished, bought again or stopped, where it sits in a routine, and a reason if you choose one. Dates are kept as a week (or a month for purchases), never an exact time.
  • What you tell us about a product: “How’s it going?” answers, how it felt, where you bought it and what you paid, and notes you write about a product, after personal details are removed.
  • Anonymous check-ins: how your skin feels, your stress level, recent travel, the day’s weather and the products in that day’s routine.
  • Broad context: an age range (like 40 to 49), your state or region and country, the weather (temperature, humidity, UV), the app version and a broad kind of phone (like “iPhone Pro”).
  • Looking up products: products you search for, price checks and taps on a store link, counted per product. A store tap is counted with a one-day code that can’t identify you or link one day to the next.

Never recorded: your name or account, anything dispensed by a pharmacy, anything from Apple Health, warning signs from a check-in or its own note, and your period or life stage.

We combine this with information from other members to learn which products people keep using and which they give up. We may share or sell these product-level insights. They never include anything that identifies you.

Who helps us run SkinOracle

These service providers run parts of SkinOracle for us. We share only what each one needs for its job:

  • Supabase (United States): our database, sign-in and server functions.
  • SkinOracle’s AI engine, provided by an AI service provider: writes Ask SkinOracle™ answers and reads scanned product labels, from the information described above.
  • Apple: App Store payments, Sign in with Apple, and the weather service.
  • Google: only if you choose to sign in with Google on this website.
  • Resend: sends our emails, like your sign-in code.
  • GitHub Pages serves this website, and Cloudflare provides the bot check on some forms. Like any web host, they see your internet (IP) address and browser type to deliver the page and stop abuse.

Our servers also see your internet (IP) address when you connect. We use it to keep SkinOracle secure and to stop abuse (for rate limits we keep only a scrambled, salted version), never to profile you.

Retail links

SkinOracle may show stores that sell a product, such as Amazon, Sephora or Ulta Beauty. If you follow a link, the store can tell the visit came from SkinOracle and may pay us a commission if you make a purchase. We don’t give stores your name, email, health details or anything else about you, and what you do on their site is covered by their own privacy policy.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell or rent your personal information, ever.
  • We don’t use advertising trackers, tracking pixels or third-party cookies, and this website uses no analytics.
  • We don’t let brands pay for access to you.

The waitlist and the beta application

  • Waitlist: your email, your first name if you give it, your consent, your invite link and how many friends joined with it. We use your email only to write to you about SkinOracle. Perks (early beta access, your name in the credits if you say yes) have no cash value.
  • Beta application: your first name, email, your iPhone model and your answers about your skincare habits, so Roxy can choose the 100 beta testers. A “tell me at launch” sign-up keeps only your email.
  • Unsubscribe any time with the link in any email. Deleting your account also deletes your waitlist sign-up and beta application for the same, confirmed email.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything in it: until you delete your account.
  • Friends chat messages: 30 days. The Ask chat in this browser: 30 days.
  • Photos you send to Friends: 30 days, then they are deleted. Circles posts: until you delete your account (then their words are removed).
  • Anonymous product information: it isn’t linked to you, so it stays when you delete your account.
  • Waitlist: until early access is over, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account right in the app: on the iPhone, You › SkinOracle account › Delete my account; on this website, Privacy and your data › Delete my account. You type DELETE to confirm. Everything we keep for your account goes, and you get a receipt number. If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to disconnect SkinOracle from your Apple ID (you can check in your iPhone’s Settings › your name › Sign in with Apple).
  • Download your data: on the iPhone, You › Privacy › Export my data; on this website, Privacy and your data › Download my data.
  • Change your mind: switch off Ask SkinOracle™, “may use my health details”, location or Apple Health at any time.
  • Your membership is billed by Apple: deleting your account doesn’t cancel it. Cancel it in your Apple subscriptions.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California, Washington or the EU/UK), you may have more rights. We honor them for everyone. Write to hello@skinoracle.ai and we’ll answer within 30 days.

Age

SkinOracle is for adults, 18 and over. We ask your date of birth on your device before you make an account. If you’re under 18, no account is made, nothing is sent to us, and your device remembers the answer for 30 days. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it. A teen version will only come after a separate legal and privacy review.

Not medical advice

SkinOracle offers wellness guidance, not medical advice. It never diagnoses, and it tells you clearly when something is worth showing a doctor.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above, and for anything significant we’ll tell you in the app or by email first. Questions? hello@skinoracle.ai.
