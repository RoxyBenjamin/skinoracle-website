Privacy policy

Last updated October 17, 2026

SkinOracle is building a skincare companion that puts you first, and that starts with how we handle your information. This policy covers only the waitlist on this website. The app will have its own, more detailed policy (including a consumer health data section) before it launches.

The short version

  • We collect your email, and anything else you choose to tell us on the form. We never ask for health information on the waitlist.
  • We use it only to tell you about early access and to understand who SkinOracle is for.
  • We never sell it. We never share it for advertising.
  • Email us and we’ll delete it. That’s it.

“What’s happening in your life?” is a broad category you pick yourself, such as “Getting married”, “Busy season” or “Life is changing”. None of the choices are about health. Please don’t send us medical or health details on the waitlist. We don’t need them, and the app asks those questions on your phone instead.

Inviting friends

After you join, you get a personal link you can share if you want to. Sharing is up to you: use the copy button or your phone’s own share menu. We never ask for your contacts, and we never send messages for you.

  • When a friend joins with your link, we store your link’s code next to their sign-up. That’s how we count them. Each friend counts once.
  • Your friend never sees your email or name on our site, and you never see theirs. You can only see how many friends joined.
  • Perks are early beta access (3 friends) and your name in the app’s credits (5 friends, only if you say yes). They have no cash value.
  • If you ask us to delete your information, your links are deleted too. Friends who joined with them stay on the list, without any record of who invited them.

In the app: product and usage information (not personal)

To build the most useful guide to which skincare products actually work, the SkinOracle app shares information about products, never about you:

  • Product details: the barcode, name, brand, type, size and ingredients, a cropped photo of the product (never of a person) and the text on its label.
  • How products are used: when a product is added, opened, used (the date and hour of each use), paused, finished, bought again or stopped, and a reason if you choose one.
  • Conditions at the time: the weather (temperature, humidity and UV) and the city, state and country where it was used (never a street address or exact location).
  • Who used it, broadly: an age range (for example 40 to 49) and, only if you allow health details when you sign up, a life stage (for example pregnancy) and cycle phase. Never your name.
  • Kind of phone: for example “iPhone Pro”, and its iOS version.

This information is sent without your name, email, account, phone serial number or any other identifier, so it isn’t linked to who you are. We combine it with information from other members to learn which products people keep using and which they give up, and we may share or sell these product insights. Your name, email, phone number, account, check-ins, notes and face photos are never part of it. Your face photos and Photo Diary are always yours. Our AI analyses each face photo to track your skin; the photo is never kept or used to train AI.

SkinOracle is your personal skin care app, diary, and skin knowledge app. We keep anonymized information about the products you use and how you use them. We keep your opinions about products so we can provide you information on how to go about your skin care routine.

Membership payments

Your membership (7 days free, then monthly) is paid through Apple. Apple handles the payment and your card details; SkinOracle never sees them. Apple tells the app only whether your membership is active.

Links to our retail partners

SkinOracle may show products you can get from retail partners such as Amazon, Sephora or Ulta Beauty. If you follow a link, the retailer can tell the visit came from SkinOracle and may pay us a commission if you make a purchase. We don’t give retailers your name, email, health details or anything else about you, and what you do on their site is covered by their own privacy policy.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell or rent your personal information, ever. (Product and usage information that isn’t linked to you is described above.)
  • We don’t use advertising trackers, tracking pixels or third-party cookies on this site.
  • We don’t let brands pay for access to you.

Analytics

Right now this site uses no analytics. If we add page statistics later, we will use a privacy-friendly, cookieless tool (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) that does not track you across sites, and we’ll update this page first.

Where it’s stored

Waitlist sign-ups are stored in a database run by our provider Supabase, hosted in the United States. Access is limited to the SkinOracle team. The website itself is served by Cloudflare. Both act as our service providers and may only process data on our instructions.

How long we keep it

Until the app launches and early access is over, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first. We’ll ask before moving you from the waitlist to anything else.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe any time using the link in any email.
  • Access, correct or delete your information: email hello@skinoracle.ai from the address you signed up with. We’ll confirm within 30 days, usually much sooner.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California, Washington or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights. We honor them for everyone.

Children

The SkinOracle waitlist is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has signed up, email us and we will delete it. A future Teen mode (13–17) will only launch after a separate legal and privacy review.

Not medical advice

SkinOracle offers wellness guidance, not medical advice. It never diagnoses. We’re building it to tell you clearly when something is worth showing a doctor.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above, and for anything significant, we’ll email you first. Questions? hello@skinoracle.ai.
