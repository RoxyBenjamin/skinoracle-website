Skin games · one minute each

Play. Learn something. Glow.

Three one-minute games about the three things that matter more than any price tag: layering in the right order, reading past the price, and sticking to a routine.

Game i · Layer it right

Tap your morning steps in the order you’d put them on.

  1. Your order shows here.

The rule of thumb: cleanser, then the thinnest to the thickest (toner, serum, moisturizer), sunscreen last in the morning. SkinOracle puts your own products in this order for you.

Game iii · The routine meter

Be honest: how many nights a week do you really do your routine?

Halfway there.

Most products need steady use to show anything. Making it most nights is where results usually begin.

What each game teaches

The lessons, in one line each.

  1. i

    Layer it right

    Cleanser first, then thinnest to thickest (toner, serum, moisturizer), and sunscreen last in the morning.

  2. ii

    The price check

    Two products with the same key ingredients at about the same strength do about the same job, whatever the jar costs.

  3. iii

    The routine meter

    Most products need steady use, most nights, before they show anything. Consistency matters more than any price tag.
