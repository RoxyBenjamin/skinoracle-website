How it works

We survey prices to get you the best deal, not because we’re paid to.

  1. iYou type a product. We ask retailers’ official sources (their own price feeds and APIs) for it. We never copy prices from websites.
  2. iiA small AI model reads the results only to keep the same product and leave out look-alikes, kits and refills. It never sets or changes a price.
  3. iiiWe show the five lowest prices, sorted by price per ml (or per gram) first, then by the total price. Never by commission.
  4. ivEach price shows when it was checked, and none is more than a day old. Prices can change; the retailer’s site price applies.
  5. vLinks open the retailer’s own site. We don’t track what you look at, and there are no price alerts.

Some offer links may earn SkinOracle a commission, and every price list says so. It never changes the order. How we stay independent →

Questions people ask

How are the prices sorted?

By price only: the lowest price per ml (or per gram) first, then the lowest total price. Whether a retailer pays us a commission never changes the order.

Does SkinOracle earn money from these links?

Some offer links may earn us a commission. We say so next to every price list, and it never changes what we show: we survey prices to get you the best deal, not because we’re paid to.

Are the prices always right?

They are checked within the last 24 hours and show when. Prices can change; the retailer’s site price applies.

How to put it on your phone

You opened this page inside another app. Tap ⋯ or Share and choose Open in Safari (or Open in Chrome) first, then follow the steps below.
