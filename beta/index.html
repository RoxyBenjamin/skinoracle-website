How the beta works

Seven steps, four weeks.

The beta is how SkinOracle gets better before everyone else gets it: you try it in your real life, and tell us what you notice.

  1. i

    Apply

    About 5 minutes. Tell us about you, your iPhone and your skin-care life.

  2. ii

    Roxy reads every application

    She picks the 100 testers herself: people who really care about their skin, with a good mix of skin, ages and routines.

  3. iii

    You get an email invitation

    If you’re picked, the invitation arrives by email with everything you need.

  4. iv

    Install TestFlight, then SkinOracle

    Install Apple’s free TestFlight app from the App Store, then open the invitation link on your iPhone to install the SkinOracle beta. TestFlight is how Apple lets people try apps before they’re in the App Store.

  5. v

    Use SkinOracle most days for 4 weeks

    Your real routine, with the products you already own. Your photos stay on your phone.

  6. vi

    Send your suggestions

    From Help & feedback inside the app, or take a screenshot in the beta: TestFlight lets you send it to us with a note. Plus a 2-minute check-in each week.

  7. vii

    Join the community

    The beta testers’ corner in the SkinOracle community (coming soon): share what you notice with other testers and with Roxy.

Questions

Before you apply.

Is the beta free?

Yes. The beta is free, and so is Apple’s TestFlight app.

Which iPhones can I use?

An iPhone that runs iOS 17 or later, including iPhone Duo.

What happens to my data?

Your application is used only to choose testers and to write to you about the beta. Your skin photos stay on your phone. Read our Privacy page.

Can I leave the beta?

Anytime. Delete the app, or leave the beta in TestFlight. If you’d like your application deleted too, just email hello@skinoracle.ai.

Who picks the testers?

Roxy, SkinOracle’s founder, reads every application herself. It isn’t first come, first served.
