6. Products you use

SkinOracle is your personal skin care app, diary, and skin knowledge app. We keep anonymized information about the products you use and how you use them. We keep your opinions about products so we can provide you information on how to go about your skin care routine.

Your name, email, phone number and account are never attached to it. Your personal details stay private. We only share them if you say so (for example, the name you pick for your friends’ groups).

What we keep about products:

  • the product: its barcode, name, brand, type and ingredients;
  • a photo of its packaging and the words on its label (never a photo of you, and never names or phone numbers from a label);
  • how you use it: when and how often, the weather, your city, your age range, your phone model, and when you open, finish or stop it and why;
  • what you think of it: love it, OK, or not for me;
  • only if you said yes to health details: your life stage and cycle phase.

To work out what a product is, we may send its photo to an AI company that works for us, such as Anthropic.

Product photos you send become part of SkinOracle’s knowledge base. We may share or sell what we learn about products, but never anything that says who you are. This helps everyone to identify bad or even dangerous products. Product facts that come from Open Beauty Facts stay free for everyone, under their license.
