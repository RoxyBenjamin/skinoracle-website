3. What SkinOracle is, and what it isn’t

SkinOracle helps you understand and organise your skincare: the products on your shelf, your routine, how your skin changes over time, and what may be affecting it.

It is wellness and education, not medical care. Nothing in SkinOracle is a medical opinion, advice, diagnosis, treatment or recommendation, and SkinOracle does not endorse any. It is not a substitute for a doctor, dermatologist or pharmacist. If you have a medical question, always consult a doctor. Always follow the directions and warnings on a product’s own label. Patch-test new products. If your skin is painful, bleeding, spreading, changing quickly or worrying you, or if you have a reaction, stop using the product and see a doctor. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Some information comes from computers and other sources, and it can be wrong. SkinOracle uses artificial intelligence (on your iPhone, including Apple Intelligence, and SkinOracle’s AI engine on our servers), product databases (such as Open Beauty Facts) and the text it reads from your photos. SkinOracle automatically fills in the product details it finds. These details can be wrong or incomplete. Use Change to correct a field, and follow the directions and warnings on the product’s own label. We don’t promise that any information is complete, current or correct.

We don’t sell skincare products, and we don’t endorse any brand. Mentions of products, brands or shops are for information only.

5. Your photos and personal information

Your face photos are looked at on your iPhone, by SkinOracle’s own rules, not by AI. They’re never sent to an AI company and never used to train AI. Each photo you keep (of you or of a product) is saved once, inside SkinOracle on your iPhone. It is not saved to your camera roll, and SkinOracle never looks at your other photos. You can export all your photos at any time (Photo Diary › Export all my photos). Photos of you are only shared when you choose photos and send them. Your answers to “Did I get that right?” are sent anonymously, never with your photo, name or account, to help SkinOracle get better. How we handle personal information is explained in our Privacy Policy at skinoracle.ai/privacy, which is part of these terms.

Ask SkinOracle™ (the chat). When you send a question to Ask SkinOracle™, your question, recent messages, remembered notes, shelf details and relevant safety information go to SkinOracle’s AI engine to write the answer. The iPhone also sends today’s routine product names. Your skin type and concerns are included when “Skin type” is on. If you attach a photo, the photo itself stays on your iPhone: only the words SkinOracle reads on a product label in it are sent (never names or phone numbers from a label). We don’t attach your account or your email address, but words or photos you send may identify you, so don’t include anything you don’t want to share. Everything else you record (your check-ins and notes) stays on your iPhone unless you send it, or choose to mirror your iPhone to your computer: then your check-ins (never the notes you write in them on your iPhone) are locked on your iPhone first and copied to your own health vault, which only you can unlock.

Why we ask for your name, email address, postal address and phone number. If you create an account, subscribe or contact us, we may ask for these details. We use them solely for communication between SkinOracle and you: for example, to send your sign-in codes, to answer your questions, to tell you about your account or subscription, and, only if you sign up for them, to send our newsletters.

We never sell your name, email address or any other personal information, and we never use it, rent it or share it for marketing by anyone else.

We keep your personal information separate from product and usage information, and we encrypt it when it is sent and when it is stored. We work hard to protect it, but no system is completely secure, so we can’t promise that it can never be accessed without permission. If that ever happens, we will tell you as the law requires.

6. Products you use

SkinOracle is your personal skin care app, diary, and skin knowledge app. We keep anonymized information about the products you use and how you use them. We keep your opinions about products so we can provide you information on how to go about your skin care routine.

Your name, email, phone number and account are never attached to it. Your personal details stay private. We only share them if you say so (for example, the name you pick for your friends’ groups).

What we keep about products:

  • the product: its barcode, name, brand, type and ingredients;
  • a photo of its packaging and the words on its label (never a photo of you, and never names or phone numbers from a label);
  • how you use it: when and how often, the weather, your region, your age range, your phone model, and when you open, finish or stop it and why;
  • what you think of it: love it, OK, or not for me;

To work out what a product is, we may send its photo to SkinOracle’s AI engine.

Product photos you send become part of SkinOracle’s knowledge base. We may share or sell what we learn about products, but never anything that says who you are. This helps everyone to identify bad or even dangerous products. Product facts that come from Open Beauty Facts stay free for everyone, under their license.

Because none of this is linked to you, we can’t find it and delete it later.
