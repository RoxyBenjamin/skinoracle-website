NEWS · 15 November 2026

New study looks at nail changes and GLP-1 receptor agonists

A new study in the British Journal of Dermatology looked at nail changes linked with a group of drugs called GLP-1 receptor agonists. It was a case-control study run in several countries. Its title points to an association, to confounding, and to some uncertainty that remains. We only have the title, so we cannot share what it found.

Why it matters for your skin: If you notice changes in your nails, please see your doctor. One study alone cannot explain why a change happens.

Source: British Journal of Dermatology

NEWS · 14 November 2026

New study looks at how common long-lasting hand eczema is in US adults

Researchers studied how many adults in the US report having chronic hand eczema. They looked at people who said a health care professional had diagnosed it. The study is a cross-sectional one, which means it gives a snapshot of one point in time.

Why it matters for your skin: Knowing how common hand eczema is can help you feel less alone. If your hands bother you, please see your doctor.

SkinOracle’s take

Here's my take, and I'll keep it simple. Long-lasting hand eczema is not a cosmetic or skincare issue. It is a health issue, and it deserves your doctor's attention. I like that the researchers focused on adults who said a health care professional had diagnosed it. That fits my point nicely. This belongs with a health care professional, not on a bathroom shelf. The study is also a snapshot of one point in time, so think of it as a picture of the moment, not the whole story. So here is my one friendly ask. Before you apply any skincare product to your hands, please see your doctor first. Skincare is lovely for lots of things, but this is one where the first step is a conversation with someone who can look at your skin and your situation. Be kind to your hands, and let the right person guide you!

SkinOracle’s commentary: opinion, not medical advice.

Source: JAMA Dermatology
