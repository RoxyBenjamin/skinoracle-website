Roxy’s rotation · 13 November 2026

What’s in my rotation right now.

I own 167 skincare products. That number was true when I typed it. By the time you read this, a box has probably arrived.

People ask me what I actually use, and the honest answer is: it rotates. My skin is naturally flushed and a little dramatic, so I lean on things that calm it down, keep my barrier happy, and brighten without picking a fight. These are the products that keep earning their spot right now.

No one paid me to be on this list. No affiliate links, no free-PR-box guilt. Just what’s in my hands most nights, and one that got voted off the island.

  1. i

    No. 1

    Anua PDRN Collagen Melting Patches + Mixsoon Melting Collagen Film

    This is my favorite part of the night, and also the part where I look the most ridiculous. The Anua patches go on my cheeks right after toner. At the same time, the Mixsoon film goes on my forehead and neck. Then I sit there for 10 to 15 minutes while they dissolve into my skin, looking like a very well-hydrated mummy.

    I don’t think of these as miracle workers. They’re the hydration and calming step that makes everything else feel better, especially on nights after a device session. I use them three or four times a week.

    Why it stays: Plump, calm skin the next morning, and 15 minutes where I’m not allowed to do anything else.

  2. ii

    No. 2

    Nano Recipe EGF + FGF Liposome Concentrate

    It’s an 11-gram bottle that does more than products ten times its size. It’s a fragrance-free growth-factor serum (EGF and FGF, delivered in liposomes), and it slots in right after my essences and before anything heavier.

    Where it really shines is after microneedling. That’s when my skin is at its most “please be gentle with me,” and this is exactly what I want to give it. I use it morning or night.

    Why it stays: Small, quiet, no fragrance, and it’s the first thing I reach for when my skin needs to recover.

  3. iii

    No. 3

    Medicube AGE-R Booster Pro

    I have a drawer of devices. Most of them were bought on a wave of optimism that lasted about a week. The Booster Pro is the one that actually comes out of the drawer.

    It’s an at-home RF and EMS device, and I use it regularly as part of my firming routine. One rule I keep: it stays away from the corners of my nose, which is where my skin gets the most reactive. On red, angry days it takes the night off, and so do I.

    Why it stays: I actually use it. For a device, there’s no higher compliment.

  4. iv

    No. 4

    Anua Azelaic Acid 10% Hyaluron Redness Serum

    My face is naturally flushed. It’s not a breakout or a flare, it’s just my baseline, and most “redness” products either do nothing or make it worse.

    This one combines azelaic acid with hyaluronic acid. It’s gentle enough that I can use it morning or night, and even on my nose, which almost nothing else gets to touch. I don’t have to plan my week around it the way I do with stronger actives.

    Why it stays: A real active that my most sensitive skin actually tolerates.

  5. v

    No. 5

    Differ & Deeper PLLT-ra Firming Band Cream

    It’s a heat-activated balm that warms as you work it in. It’s my nighttime step for the jawline, neck and cheeks, the places that apparently got the memo that I’m in my forties.

    I massage it in while watching TV, slowly and with great seriousness, like it’s a ritual. Because it is. Even if the firming were only half as good as the label says, the five minutes of massage would be worth it.

    Why it stays: It turns my least favorite skincare goal into my favorite part of the evening.

  6. vi

    No. 6

    Blancmuse Le Blanc UV Protective Fluid SPF50+ PA++++

    Everything above is pointless if you skip this one. It’s the last step of every morning, right before makeup.

    It’s a light fluid that sits on top of a ten-layer routine without making a fuss. That’s honestly the bar most sunscreens fail to clear.

    Why it stays: I wear it every single day. Unlike my four chickens, who refuse.

Voted off the island

Cutem Clioly Botul-Thera Lifting Gua Sha Cream Stick

In the spirit of honesty: not everything makes it. This was a lifting cream in a stick with a gua sha edge built in. It sounded like two steps in one, and I love a shortcut.

I just didn’t like it, so it’s out of the rotation. That doesn’t make it a bad product, it just wasn’t right for my skin. But you deserve to hear about the misses too, not just the wins someone paid to promote.

The real secret

None of these is magic on its own. What made the difference for me was knowing what I own, what goes with what, and when to back off. That’s exactly why I built SkinOracle.

Check back next month. The list will have changed, and so will the product count.

Roxy, written in San Francisco under close supervision from 4 chickens, 2 Aussies and 2 foster pigeons.

This is Roxy’s own routine, for her skin. What works for her may not suit yours: try one new thing at a time, patch test, and follow each product’s and device’s directions.
