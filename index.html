Featured

Winter is here: small habits that keep dry skin comfortable

Cold air outdoors and heating indoors both pull water out of your skin. Dermatologists suggest short, warm (not hot) showers, a gentle fragrance-free cleanser, and patting skin dry instead of rubbing. Moisturize within a few minutes, while skin is still damp: a cream or ointment holds in more water than a lotion. A humidifier at home and gloves outdoors help too.

Why it matters: What you do right after every wash matters more for winter skin than any single product.

American Academy of Dermatology · 13 November 2026

SPLIT THE DAY

Vitamin C in the morning, retinoid at night.

Yes, just not at the same time. They work best at different times of day: vitamin C in the morning, under your sunscreen, and retinol in the evening. Used together they can be harsher on skin and each works less well.
