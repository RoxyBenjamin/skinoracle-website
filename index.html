How it works

A skin companion that starts with your life, not your skin.

Most apps stop at a quiz. SkinOracle keeps learning, explains every recommendation, and often tells you to buy less.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Your life stage, quick check-ins, photos and the products you already own.

  2. 02

    Understand

    Patterns across hormones, sleep, travel and weather, in your skin, not an average.

  3. 03

    Explain

    Every step says why, in plain words. No black boxes.

  4. 04

    Guide

    Routines from 5 minutes to a full hour of pampering, built from your shelf.

  5. 05

    Evolve

    As your life and skin change, your routine changes with them.

Then back to Observe. The loop is the product.

Who it’s for

For every chapter of life.

Every gender, every adult age, every faith and budget, with a teen mode coming later. SkinOracle adapts its questions to who you are. It suggests; it never assumes.

  • The bride or groom

    A countdown skin plan to the big day, with a product freeze before it.

  • Going through chemo

    An extra-gentle mode for cancer treatment. Your care team comes first, always.

  • On hormones

    Guidance for skin on testosterone or estrogen, without assumptions.

  • New parents

    Pregnancy-aware checks and 5-minute routines for very short nights.

  • The minimalist

    Two products and done. And that’s enough.

  • Teens 13–17

    Simple routines and learning first. No product pushing, ever.

Our principles · in writing

Everyone else is selling skincare. We’re teaching it.

Customer first. Always. Trust is the product.

We promise to

  • Explain every recommendation
  • Keep your health data private
  • Point you to a doctor when it matters
  • Work for every skin and body

We will never

  • Sell sponsored routines
  • Sell your health data
  • Market products to kids

Ethical AI isn’t a feature. It’s how we build.

  • Explainable. Every recommendation shows its reasons.
  • Human-guided. Dermatologists will sign off on the guardrails. The AI never diagnoses.
  • Your data, your call. Your answers are private. We never sell your personal data. Export or delete it anytime.
  • Fair by design. Fairness across skin tones, genders and ages is part of our review plan, and any photo features will be tested across all skin tones before they ship.
  • No hidden incentives. No brand can pay to change what we recommend.
  • Knows its limits. We’re building it to tell you clearly when something is worth showing a doctor.

Why I built this

Why I built this, a note from Roxy

“I went looking for an app that could manage what I have, show me how to use it and teach me along the way. It didn’t exist. So I’m building it.”

I’m not the founder you’d expect. That’s the point. I taught myself to code at 13 so I could run a fan newsletter. Then I spent 17 years as an executive assistant to some of the brightest minds, watching from the inside how great companies get built.

And I’ve always been a skincare lover: K-beauty, devices, the full ritual, and a cabinet that kept growing. I saw incredible science, and influencers selling anything and everything for a commission. No one was looking out for the person holding the bottle.

I spent my career making brilliant people better at what they do. Now I’m doing that for everyone’s skin.
