Why I built this

It didn’t exist. So I’m building it.

“I went looking for an app that could manage what I have, show me how to use it and teach me along the way. It didn’t exist. So I’m building it.” Roxy, founder of SkinOracle

A note from Roxy

For 17 years I’ve been an executive assistant. I’m the person who knows where everything is, what it’s for, and what breaks if you skip a step. Before that, I was a 13-year-old teaching myself to code so I could run a Hanson fan newsletter. Bringing order to chaos for people I care about has been my whole personality since “MMMBop.”

Then I turned that eye on my own bathroom cabinet. K-beauty serums, a Medicube booster, a microneedling pen, the full ritual. Last count: 167 products. (That number is accurate as of this morning and is almost certainly wrong by now.) At some point it stopped being a routine and turned into a collection I couldn’t keep straight. What goes with what? What cancels out what? Is that device in the drawer actually doing anything?

The science is real, and honestly thrilling. But most of the advice out there comes from people paid to sell you the next bottle. No one was looking out for the person actually holding it.

So I built what I couldn’t find: something that knows what you own, shows you how to use it and teaches you why, with no commission riding on the answer.

I spent my career making brilliant people better at what they do. SkinOracle is me doing that for your skin.

RoxyRoxy Benjamin, founder

Built in San Francisco with help from 4 backyard chickens, 2 dogs and 2 foster pigeons, none of whom use SPF.
