Little tips, big difference

Skin-care tips, with the reason for each.

Most good skin care is small habits done every day: the right order, the right amount, patience with new products and sunscreen every morning. Here’s each tip, and why it helps.

Today’s tip

Patch test something new before it goes on your whole face.

A small spot along the jaw or behind the ear for a few days shows how your skin takes it.

All tips

  1. i

    Thinnest to thickest: water-light first, rich cream last.

    Thin layers sink in first; heavier creams sit on top and help hold everything in.

  2. ii

    Give a new product weeks, not days, before you judge it.

    Skin renews over about a month. Most products need steady use before they show anything.

  3. iii

    Two strong actives on the same night? Usually one too many.

    Retinoids and exfoliating acids each do a lot. Together they often mean redness and peeling.

  4. iv

    Moisturize while skin is still slightly damp.

    It helps hold in the water that’s already on your skin.

  5. v

    Sunscreen: two finger-lengths for face and neck.

    Most people use far less than the amount sunscreens are tested with, so they get less protection than the label says.

  6. vi

    Outdoors all day? Reapply sunscreen every two hours.

    And after swimming or sweating. Morning sunscreen doesn’t last until evening.

  7. vii

    Wash your face with lukewarm water, not hot.

    Hot water strips more of your skin’s own oils, and skin feels tighter afterwards.

  8. viii

    Start one new product at a time.

    If your skin reacts, you’ll know which one it was.

  9. ix

    Pat your face dry; don’t rub.

    Less friction is kinder to your skin’s barrier.

  10. x

    Your neck and the backs of your hands count too.

    They see as much sun as your face. Take your routine a little further down.

  11. 11

    Wash pillowcases and makeup brushes often.

    They collect oil, makeup and bacteria that end up back on your skin.
