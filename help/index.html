Common questions

What is SkinOracle?

A calm skincare companion for iPhone. It reads the shelf you already own, explains every step of your routine, and often suggests using less. It offers wellness guidance, not medical advice. There's no Android app yet, but your account, shelf and health vault also open on skinoracle.ai in any browser.

Do I need an account?

Yes, just your email address: we send you a sign-in code. Your account also lets your shelf and settings follow you to the website and your other devices, and keeps your founding-member perks if you joined the waitlist. Shelf sync is off until you turn it on, and health details never sync that way.

How do I sign in?

Use Sign in with Apple, Google, or a one-time code sent to your email. There's no SkinOracle password to remember for your account. If you used Apple's Hide My Email, your account uses that private address.

How do founding-member perks work?

If you joined the waitlist, sign in with the same email and your perks come with you. When 3 friends join with your personal link you get early access to the iPhone beta, and at 5 friends your name can go in the app's thank-you credits, only if you'd like it there.

What is the health vault?

It's a locked space for your health details, like your life stage, medications, cycle, check-ins and patterns. They're locked on your phone or in your browser before they're sent, so SkinOracle can't read them. Only you can open the vault, with your vault password, your recovery code or a passkey.

I forgot my vault password. Can you reset it?

We can't, because we never hold the key. Your recovery code or a passkey you added opens the vault too, so try those first, then choose a new password. If all of them are lost, the health history in the vault can't be recovered. Your account, shelf and perks aren't affected, and you can set up a new vault.

What does SkinOracle do with my data?

As little as possible. Your health details stay on your phone unless you turn on the locked vault. Your face photos and your Photo Diary are always yours: they stay in your Photos app or SkinOracle's Files folder, and only you decide if you send one. Our AI analyses each face photo to track your skin; the photo is never kept or used to train AI. Product details (like a product's barcode, ingredients and a photo of the product) and anonymous product-use records (which product, roughly when, your city and age range) go to SkinOracle's product database without your name. We never sell anything that says who you are. We don't show ads or use tracking tools from other companies, and our emails don't track opens or clicks.

How do I get a copy of my data, or delete it?

On your phone, go to You › Privacy and choose Export my data or Delete everything. On the website, sign in and open Privacy and your data. Delete my account removes everything online too: your synced shelf and settings, the vault, your perks, messages you sent us while signed in, and your newsletter subscription. You get a receipt when it's done, and both are always free.

Why didn't a product scan?

SkinOracle looks barcodes up in Open Beauty Facts, a free product list that keeps growing. Newer or smaller brands may be missing. You can add the product by hand with its name and main ingredients, and it works the same way on your shelf.

When will I see patterns?

SkinOracle waits until your check-ins show something often enough to be worth mentioning, so a pattern can take a few weeks to appear. It shows at most one new pattern a week, always with the counts behind it, and it quietly retires a pattern that stops holding up.

Can SkinOracle tell me what's going on with my skin?

No. SkinOracle is about everyday routines and habits, not medical care, and it never names what a spot or rash is. When you mention a warning sign, it tells you who to see and how soon. For anything that worries you, a dermatologist or your doctor is the right person. If your face, lips or eyes swell, or breathing feels hard, call 911 in the US.

What are Circles?

Circles are coming soon: small, calm groups where members share what felt good for their skin, like travel, sensitive skin or perimenopause. You'll pick a circle name, so nobody sees your real name or email, and nothing from your vault is ever shared. Circles are for adults, 18 and over.

Will you email me?

Only if you ask. Newsletters are separate from the waitlist and you pick the topics: product news, skin tips or Roxy's notes. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe and a link to change your topics.

How do I contact you?

Use Send feedback or Contact us in Help, or email hello@skinoracle.ai. A person reads every message, and as a small team we can take a few days to reply. Nothing is attached to your message unless you choose it, and we never see your vault. If something is urgent, call 911 or your local emergency number.
