    Instructions for other phones and browsers
    iPhone

    Install on iPhone

    1. Open skinoracle.ai/pricer in Safari (in Chrome on iPhone: the Share button ⬆︎ is at the top right, in the address bar).
    2. Tap the Share button ⬆︎ (bottom of the screen, or top right on some iPhones).
    3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. If you see Open as Web App, leave it switched on.
    4. Tap Add. The SO Pricer icon appears on your Home Screen.
    5. Open it from the icon. It runs full-screen, like an app. The first time, allow the camera.

    Works on iPhone XS and newer with a current iOS.

    Android

    Install on Android

    1. Open skinoracle.ai/pricer in Chrome.
    2. Tap Install on this phone above, or open Chrome's ⋮ menu and tap Install app (on some phones: Add to Home screen → Install).
    3. Open SO Pricer from your Home screen or app drawer and allow the camera.

    Samsung Internet: menu → Add page to → Home screen.

    How it works

    Using Product Pricer

    1. Tap Scan a product. Photograph the front label, then the ingredients and the barcode or QR code (each optional: tap Skip).
    2. Wait a few seconds while it lifts the product off the background, reads the barcode and finds prices.
    3. You see the product's name and up to three prices, each with the store and a link.
    4. Tap Save to my phone. On iPhone choose Save Images (Photos) or Save to Files and pick (or create) a folder called Skin Oracle. Files remembers it next time.
    5. Every scan also stays in Product Pricer under My scans.

    Why one tap to save? Phones never let a website put files into your Photos or Files on its own. You always choose.

    For the best pictures

    Fill most of the frame with the product, in good light, without glare on shiny labels. A plain background gives the cleanest cut-out. Hold the barcode flat and close.

    What SkinOracle keeps

    Facts about the product: its cut-out pictures, name, ingredients, barcode and prices, the store you bought it at if you say, and the area you scanned it in (about 10 km across, never the exact spot). No name, email or account is needed or kept, and nothing links a scan to you. Please photograph products, not people. Prescription medicines are never kept. Prices come from public store pages at the moment you scan and can change, so always check the store's page. More in the Terms of Use and Privacy pages.
