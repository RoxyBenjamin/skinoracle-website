If I Were Penny, I’d Reach for the Zinc Oxide

SAN FRANCISCO · NOVEMBER 13, 2026

My chicken Penny is molting, and the poor girl looks miserable. Feathers everywhere, mood somewhere near the floor. I wrote this from San Francisco with a cup of tea and a lot of sympathy.

The thing that got me most is her comb. It's normally a proud, bright red, and right now it's pale and dry. She looks like she's had a very long week and nobody brought her snacks.

So here is my honest opinion. If I were a chicken, aka Penny, I would reach for my zinc oxide. I'm no chicken doctor, and I'm certainly not suggesting you smooth anything onto your flock. This is just what I'd do in her little feathery shoes.

Why zinc oxide? It's the kind of ingredient you often find in gentle, protective creams, the sort of thing people like to have around when skin feels dry and a bit unhappy. It's a comfort-blanket sort of ingredient, and a pale, dry comb looks like it could use one.

Now for my favorite fun fact. Chickens can lose up to 20% of their body weight during a molt. That's one fifth of the whole bird! It's basically the chicken version of PMS, a big, dramatic, slightly grumpy reset that leaves everybody looking a little rough around the edges.

And I have to ask, why can't the same happen for me? A little seasonal shedding, a fifth of me gone, and then I strut back out glossy and fabulous? Where do I sign up? Nature clearly gave the chickens the good deal on that one.

Until that miracle arrives, I'll stick to what I can control. Pay attention to dry patches, be kind to my skin when it looks tired, and keep a soothing, protective cream within arm's reach. Penny and I have more in common than I'd like to admit.

So here's to Penny, and to every dry, droopy, pale-combed moment in life. May your feathers grow back fast, and may your skin feel like the best version of itself again.

Nano Recipe EGF + FGF Liposome Holy Grail

SAN FRANCISCO · NOVEMBER 13, 2026

Confession time. I am a little bit obsessed with the Nano Recipe EGF + FGF Liposome right now, and I'm calling it my holy grail. The weather in San Francisco can't make up its mind, and I'm packing for Paris, so my skin and I are in a very serious relationship with whatever earns a spot in the bag.

So what is it? It's a Korean liposome concentrate serum with 10ppm EGF and 10ppm FGF, 20ppm total, and a few drops go into your routine. The brand says EGF supports skin cell renewal and helps maintain surface elasticity, while FGF works in the deeper dermal layer to help maintain elasticity. Glycerin is along for the ride too, a humectant that attracts water to hydrate skin.

Now, you may have noticed I promised some science. Here's my honest take. I like that this serum has a clear idea behind it. One growth factor works on the surface, the other works deeper, so the whole skin gets a little attention instead of just the top layer. I'm not going to wave around study numbers I can't point to, because a column should never be made of fairy dust. What I can tell you is why that two-level thinking makes sense to me.

I also love how simple it is. A few drops, no big production. When I'm travelling, anything that takes the fuss out of a routine is a friend of mine. Airplane air, a new climate and a suitcase full of maybes are enough drama already.

And about that weather change. Seasons shifting always make my skin feel like it's been left on the porch overnight. Having one dependable step that I trust, and that has hydrating glycerin in the mix, makes the whole routine feel calmer. That's my opinion, not a promise, and everyone's skin has its own personality.

Okay, the chicken joke, as requested. Why did the chicken cross the road? To get to the serum on the other side. I'll see myself out. Though honestly, I'd say that's more effort than any chicken should go to for good skin, and you only need a few drops.

So that's why it's my must have for now. Next stop, Paris, with a small bottle tucked in my bag and big plans to stroll along the Seine looking effortlessly dewy. Or at least happy. Happy skin is a good start!
