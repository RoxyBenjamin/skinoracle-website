Unbiased, visibly

How we stay independent.

SkinOracle has no sponsored content and no paid placements, and no brand can pay to change what we say or recommend. Some price links may earn us a commission, and we always say so; offers are sorted by price, never by commission.

We will never

  • Sponsored content or paid placements
  • Show or order a product because a retailer pays us
  • Sell sponsored routines
  • Sell your health data
  • Market products to kids

Prices and commissions

We survey prices to get you the best deal, not because we’re paid to.

When we show where a product costs what, some of those links may earn SkinOracle a commission. That helps pay for the free tools. It never decides what you see:

  • Offers are ordered only by price: price per ml (or per g) first, then the total price. Never by commission.
  • Whether a retailer pays us never changes what we show or recommend. The code that sorts prices never even reads it.
  • Next to every price list: “We may earn a commission if you buy through these links. It never changes what we show: offers are sorted by price, lowest first.”
  • Only the top 5 prices, each from the retailer’s own official source, with the time it was checked (never older than 24 hours). Prices can change; the retailer’s site price applies.
  • Offer links are marked as sponsored links for search engines, and open the retailer’s own site.
  • No price alerts and no tracking of what you look at.

How news and recalls are chosen

  • Only trusted sources: dermatology journals, regulators (such as the FDA, Health Canada and the EU Safety Gate) and reputable news.
  • Press releases, product launches, “sponsored” and influencer pieces are left out.
  • Every summary is in our own words, with the original linked and credited. We never copy an article.
  • Roxy decides what is published. Nothing appears without her.

How facts are checked

  • Sources are listed on every science page.
  • A science page goes up only after a dermatologist has reviewed it, and the reviewer is named on the page with the review date.
  • Education, not medical advice: SkinOracle never diagnoses, and says “please see your doctor” when it matters.

Corrections

Spotted a mistake? Email hello@skinoracle.ai with “Correction” in the subject. We check it, fix the page and show the new “updated” date.
