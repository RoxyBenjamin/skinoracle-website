The science · the sun

Do I really need sunscreen every day?

Yes: daily sunscreen is the most protective step in any routine, because UV reaches skin on cloudy days and through windows, and its effects add up over years. Use enough (about two finger-lengths for face and neck), choose broad spectrum SPF 30 or higher, and reapply every two hours outdoors.

Mineral or chemical filters

  • Mineral filters (zinc oxide, titanium dioxide) and chemical filters both work. The best sunscreen is the one you’ll wear every day.

When to see a doctor

  • If a mole or spot changes in size, shape or colour, or looks different from the others, please see your doctor.

Questions people ask

Do I need sunscreen on cloudy days?

Yes. Much of the sun’s UV gets through clouds.

Do I need sunscreen indoors?

Near windows, often yes: UVA passes through most window glass. [Fact to check, with a source.]
