The science · layering

In what order should I apply skin care?

Apply skin care from the thinnest texture to the thickest, after cleansing, and finish with sunscreen in the morning. Some actives work better at different times of day, so both the order and the pairing matter.

The order

  • Cleanser, then toner or essence (optional), then serum, then moisturizer.
  • In the morning, sunscreen goes last, on top of everything.
  • Oils go after water-based layers; a very rich balm goes last at night.

Pairs that clash, and pairs that team up

  • Retinoids and exfoliating acids: better on different nights.
  • Vitamin C and a retinoid: vitamin C in the morning, the retinoid at night.
  • Retinoids or acids and sunscreen: a must-have pair.

Step by step

  1. i
    Cleanse. Wash with a gentle cleanser and lukewarm water; pat dry.
  2. ii
    Toner or essence (optional). If you use one, it goes first: it’s the thinnest layer.
  3. iii
    Serum. Your active or hydrating serum. One active per routine is plenty.
  4. iv
    Moisturizer. Seals in the layers underneath.
  5. v
    Sunscreen (morning). Last step every morning: about two finger-lengths for face and neck.

Questions people ask

Does the order of skin care really matter?

Yes. Thin, watery products sink in best on clean skin; heavier creams on top help hold everything in, and sunscreen needs to sit on the surface to protect it.

Can I use retinol and vitamin C together?

Yes, just not at the same time. They work best at different times of day: vitamin C in the morning, under your sunscreen, and retinol in the evening.
